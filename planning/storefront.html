How to make it

Six minutes, one bowl, no stove.

  1. 01

    Empty the sachet into a deep bowl.

    0:00
  2. 02

    Add 200ml water at 90°C — kettle-hot, just off the boil. Stir once, thoroughly.

    0:20
  3. 03

    Cover and leave it alone. Don't stir while it rests.

    1:00
  4. 04

    Stir through and serve. Thicken with less water, loosen with more.

Design system

The parts, and why

Nothing here is decorative. Colour carries the range, mono type carries data, and the label panel is a component because compliance is a legal duty, not a footnote.

Colour encodes the mechanic

Each range is coloured by how you make it, so the chip on a card tells you the prep method before you read a word. That's the taxonomy doing work, not a palette being pretty.

Express#E8931C

Hot water. Also the global accent — Express is the flagship range.

Hot & Fresh#C4442A

Stove heat. Doubles as the sale/urgency colour.

Flavors#3E7D5A

Cold water. Also confirmation and savings.

Ink#14201C

Green-black. Text, dark bands, primary buttons.

Paper#FBF9F5

Warm off-white ground. Not cream.

Veg mark#2E7D32

Legally specified. Never used for anything else.

Deliberately not cream-and-terracotta. That palette is where every instant-food brand ends up, and it makes the shelf life and prep-time story impossible to tell.

The label panel

FSSAI licence, net quantity, veg mark, allergens, country of origin and consumer care are legal duties for food sold online in India. Built as structured fields rather than free text, they're enforceable, they render consistently, and the same data feeds the Google Merchant Center product feed at no extra cost. Compliance paying for itself twice.

Product card

Leads with the range chip and prep time, because that's how people actually choose here — "what can I eat in six minutes" beats "what is this dish". Price and the add button sit on a baseline so a row of cards scans as a column of numbers.

One animation

Steam, on the hero bowl and the product image. It's the single most recognisable thing about the product and it earns the motion. Everything else holds still, and the whole effect disappears under prefers-reduced-motion.
