The dal you would make on a Tuesday if you had the hour: yellow moong and toor, tempered with cumin, tomato and a little ghee, then dried slowly so the tempering survives.

Built for the places a stove is not — a train berth, a hostel drawer, a hotel kettle, a desk at nine at night. One pack is dinner for two, or a very good lunch for one.
