# UX review register — 8 November 2026

A full usability review of the storefront, before launch, at phone width
(390px) and laptop width (1280px), with every finding confirmed in the
browser before it was fixed. Commit `7e15212` carries the fixes; this file
records what was found, what was done and what is still open.

## How it was done

1. **Review.** Four reviewers walked every screen of the prototype
   (`build/preview/storefront.html`, rendered from the real theme) as a
   customer would, in four areas: browse (home, shop, search, cards),
   product page, buying (cart, box, checkout, order received) and account.
   94 findings, each with a priority: **4 P0** (costs a customer money or
   an order), about 30 P1, the rest P2/P3.
2. **Fix.** Three groups with separate file ownership — look (CSS,
   templates, patterns), product page, buying + account — then one
   integration pass.
3. **Re-check.** Full gate, a click audit and screenshots at both widths.
   The re-check found five more defects (below), all fixed in the same
   commit.

The finding text lived in the reviewers' reports; those reports were not
saved to the repository. This register lists every finding ID that a fix
cites (76 of 94). The other IDs are not cited by any fix, so their
individual status is not recorded here.

## Found on re-check (all fixed in `7e15212`)

- **Cart overflowed at 390px** (527px wide). Bare `1fr` grid tracks have an
  `auto` minimum, so the cart row's name column counted at its max-content
  width. Both grids now use `minmax(0, 1fr)`. The prototype also printed two
  lines of raw template code in the cart.
- **"Delivery | Delivery: ₹59".** On the live site this would have read
  "Shipping | Delivery: ₹59". The row is headed "Delivery"
  (`foodify_shipping_heading`) and a method with that title shows only its
  price (`foodify_shipping_method_label`). checkout-test +6.
- **"Add another · 1 in cart" clipped** on two-up phone cards. The button
  may wrap, balanced; a no-break space keeps "·" on the first line.
- **Prep line stranded a "·"** at a line end; "Serves 2" now sits beside the
  chip with no separator, and wraps under it cleanly.
- **Footer links broke mid-name** ("Build a / box") at 820px and 1280px. The
  footer uses the header's wide rail, and the Shop list is one column from
  782px to 1099px.

## Verification

- Gate: **38 suites green** (`look-test` and `pdp-scripts` added to
  `run-all-tests.sh`).
- Browser sweep: every screen clean (no sideways scroll, no collisions) at
  every width.
- Click audit: about 3,500 taps per width (phone and laptop), no script
  errors. Six controls did nothing when tapped blind; each was then used as
  a customer would and works: box steppers and "Add box" are disabled until
  a size is chosen; "Notify me", the 404 search and "Send report" have
  required fields; address "Delete" waits on a confirm dialog.

## Open

- **PRODUCT-11 (partly).** Combo items link to their products; the panel
  placement under Add to cart is done. Nothing further planned unless the
  client asks.
- **PRODUCT-12 (partly).** Histogram rows are not the star filter. When
  reviews are paged the histogram shows totals (62) while the filter reaches
  only the loaded reviews; making the rows filters would bring back the
  mismatch PRODUCT-6 removed. Deliberately left.
- **BUY-9.** The coupon message is fixed; the field's own behaviour is
  WooCommerce's and needs a look on staging.
- **Header "₹599".** The strip's free-delivery figure is still typed, not
  read from the shipping setting.
- **Real-phone pass.** `DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md`: SMS code entry, keypads, a
  low-end Android on 4G, screen readers. Not testable from a laptop.

## For the client

Design calls made in the review — say if you disagree:
- Tablets (up to 899px) show the menu button rather than five links.
- On phones the filters start folded; below 1024px the SAVE badge sits
  above the price.
- Cards carry one extra line: Jain, vegan or gluten free as words.
- The phone cart button shows the item count, not the total.

Questions only the client can answer:
- **"No preservatives"** now appears on every product page. Is it true of
  every product?
- **Box savings.** At current prices five of six boxes save under 15%, so
  they show the saving in rupees but no SAVE badge.
- ~~**Unserviceable PIN.**~~ **Answered 8 Nov: block it.** Checkout opens a
  pop-up and locks Place order; the server refuses the order too. The zones
  list therefore decides where the shop sells — every state served must be
  in it.
- **Order status label.** A reported order shows "Problem reported" in
  wp-admin too. Keep that wording?
- **What a box counts in.** Boxes say "3 meals · ₹175 per meal" by default.
  The Chai & Chutney Box and the Chutney Trio Box hold chai and chutneys,
  not meals; setting their `_foodify_combo_unit` to `pack` makes them say
  "packs". Confirm, and it goes into the product data.
